A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. When it happens in Springdale, Springdale Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Why Sewage Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Springdale

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste
  • Drain openings or basement slabs coated with visible sewage residue
  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Flush nothing but toilet paper, because wipes, even 'flushable' ones, cause clogs
  • Fit a strainer over each sink and shower drain so hair and debris stay out of the pipes
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
